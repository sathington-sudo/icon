Complete Peace of Mind

Your Data Security is Guaranteed

We understand that privacy concerns keep many people from donating old hardware. At Hamilton TechReset, we offer flexible data options tailored to your specific preferences.

Option 1: NIST 800-88 Data Wipe + Serialized Certificate

Every hard drive is scrubbed using DoD standard overwrite software. You receive an official certificate listing drive serial numbers.

Option 2: Physical Hard Drive Shredding

For high-security compliance or absolute peace of mind, we physically render drives unusable and send shredding confirmation.

Option 3: Don't Require Data Erasure / Not Needed

Already wiped your drive yourself, removed the storage, or donating blank systems? Choose this to skip wiping and fast-track processing!

Why Linux Makes Old Tech Feel Like New

Modern Windows operating systems are heavy and bloated, making 5-10 year old hardware grind to a painful crawl.

By replacing outdated systems with lightweight, modern Linux, we eliminate slowdowns, block viruses, and give receivers a ultra-fast, user-friendly computer equipped with web browsers, office software, and video conferencing!
