Complete Peace of Mind

Your Data Security is Guaranteed

We understand that privacy concerns keep many people from donating old hardware. At Hamilton TechReset, we offer flexible data destruction tailored to your specific preferences.

Option 1: NIST 800-88 Data Wipe + Serialized Certificate

Every hard drive is scrubbed using DoD standard overwrite software. You receive an official certificate listing drive serial numbers.

Option 2: Physical Hard Drive Shredding

For high-security compliance or absolute peace of mind, we physically render drives unusable and send shredding confirmation.

Option 3: Don't Require Data Erasure / Not Needed

Already wiped your drive yourself, removed the storage, or donating blank systems? Choose this to skip wiping and fast-track processing!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is pickup really 100% free?

Yes! Whether you have 1 laptop at home or 20 computers at an office in Hamilton, Ancaster, Dundas, or Stoney Creek, our pickup service is completely free of charge.

What if I already wiped my hard drive or don't need data erasure?

Simply select "Don't Require Data Erasure / Not Needed" when scheduling your pickup! If you've already formatted your drive or removed it, we skip the erasure step and go straight to refurbishing.

What age or condition of laptops do you accept?

We accept working or non-working laptops and desktops, provided they aren't severely physically crushed or flooded. Even if a battery is dead or a power cable is missing, we can refurbish or harvest parts cleanly!

How does the Certificate of Data Destruction work?

When wiping drives, we record the manufacturer, model, and individual serial numbers of your storage drives. Once the 3-pass DoD sanitization finishes, we generate a PDF certificate and email it to you.
